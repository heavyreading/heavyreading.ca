NOTES

Production is not the story here, storage and pipes are. Cushing is a crossing: Canadian and Bakken barrels arrive from the north, Permian barrels from the west, Rockies and DJ barrels from the northwest, and the Gulf Coast refining centre pulls from the south. The tank farm in the middle is what lets those flows be uneven, and its stock level is the continent's pressure gauge.

The identity printed on the hub is the point of the map: Cushing is the delivery point for the NYMEX WTI futures contract. A barrel does not have to be produced anywhere near Oklahoma to be priced off what happens in these tanks.

Line width ranks the doors: width follows the published flow where one exists and capacity where only capacity is published. Every label states which it is.

Cushing is drawn as a crossing, not a field. The four inbound families are the story: the Enbridge door from Flanagan (Canada and the Bakken), the Permian door (Basin and Centurion), the Rockies and DJ door (Pony Express, Saddlehorn and Grand Mesa, White Cliffs), and the Platte barrels that arrive through Wood River and Patoka. The outbound family is smaller and mostly points one way: south to the Gulf on Seaway and Marketlink, east to Memphis on Diamond, and a short hop to the Tulsa and Ponca City refineries.

Stocks are the gauge. The EIA prints Cushing inventories every week; the October 23, 2026 print of 27.0 million barrels sits near 35 percent of the roughly 76 million barrels of working storage capacity EIA surveys at the hub. Low stocks mean the crossing is tight and inland barrels bid for space; high stocks mean barrels are stranding and inland prices pay for it.

The Bakken connection runs through Flanagan and Patoka, both drawn here: Dakota Access delivers at Patoka, and the Flanagan South and Spearhead lines are the Bakken and Canadian door into Cushing itself. The Bakken map carries that system in full.

A crude line named Cypress into or out of Cushing: none exists in the filed record. Cypress Interstate Pipeline LLC appears in the FERC Form 6 universe as a Louisiana natural gas carrier. The outbound crude set drawn here is the filed set: Seaway, Marketlink, Diamond, Ozark and the local refinery laterals.

Monthly flows by pipeline: no public source publishes them. Form 6 is annual, so flows on this map print at Form 6 grain only.

Tank by tank storage: EIA publishes Cushing stocks and working capacity in aggregate. Operator level tankage at the hub is not public at this grain.
