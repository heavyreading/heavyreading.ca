NOTES

Delivery is Regina. Kerrobert is a different hub on different systems (Plains Manito; Inter Pipeline Mid-Saskatchewan) and takes no South Saskatchewan barrels on the WS-H record.

Every number on this sheet carries its vintage. The 2002 throughput and the 2003 dimensions describe the system Plains bought, not a current flow or a current nameplate.

Catchment shading is inferred. Battery-level membership in this system is not publicly derivable (WS-H Part 3).

Delivery is Regina, not Kerrobert: the Kerrobert hub belongs to Plains' Manito system and Inter Pipeline's Mid-Saskatchewan system (WS-H secs 1.2 and 2.3). Nothing in WS-H routes South Saskatchewan barrels to Kerrobert, and no Kerrobert leg is drawn.

Fosterton is both a place and a grade: Enbridge's commodity map carries Fosterton (F) as a current heavy receipt commodity in the Premium Conventional Heavy group (effective 1 Oct 2025), so the origin name on this map is also the name the Mainline receipt system still uses for these barrels.

This is the thinnest dossier in WS-H Part 1: sec 1.3 rests on Grade C sources (a 2022 compilation of Plains system descriptions and a 2003-vintage delivery summary). The map's grain is set by that record: one named origin, one delivery terminal, two delivery outlets, and inferred catchment shading. Anything finer would be invention.

The Wascana light feeder terminates at the same PMC Regina Terminal (WS-H secs 1.4 and 8.4) and is noted here for terminal context only; it is not drawn (see withheld).

History on the corridor: the line was built for the Fosterton field (discovered Jan 1952) and was carrying about 5,000 b/d to Regina and Moose Jaw by 1955 (Saskatchewan Hansard, 21 Feb 1955); Plains acquired the system from South Saskatchewan Pipe Line in 2003 (Energy Intelligence, 23 Nov 2003). Both figures are history, printed with their vintages, and neither is a current flow.

WITHHELD

Battery-level membership is not drawn and is not publicly derivable: in the held Petrinex test (June 2026), the battery-to-pipeline counterparty is confidentiality-suppressed on 87.9% of Saskatchewan battery oil-disposition volume (WS-H Part 3). This map is drawn at system and receipt-point grain; catchment shading and the feeder edge are INFERRED, never battery membership.

System capacity is withheld because it is undisclosed, not estimated: no disclosed capacity for the South Saskatchewan System was found in the operator or CER sources WS-H checked (WS-H sec 1.3 and open item 2). nameplate_kbd therefore stays absent on every edge and on the terminal node. The only capacity number on this map is the Co-op Refinery's own 145 kb/d (WS-H sec 8.4), which is a refinery figure, not pipeline capacity.

No current flow is carried: flows_kbd is absent on every edge and no last_flow_kbd is printed. The only throughput figures in the record are historical: about 5,000 b/d from the Fosterton fields to Regina and Moose Jaw in 1955 (Saskatchewan Hansard, 21 Feb 1955) and 52,000 b/d system throughput in 2002 (Energy Intelligence, 23 Nov 2003). Following the Drayton dataset's rule, a stale figure is history in the print sheet, not a current flow, and nothing is interpolated forward from 2002.

Committed and uncommitted capacity are absent: no committed split, commitment term or shipper commitment figure is published for this system in the sources checked, so committed_kbd, uncommitted_kbd and commitment_term stay absent on every edge.

Tolls: no toll_refs are cited on any edge. tolls.db (queried 2026-10-19) holds no South Saskatchewan pipeline, path or toll rows. The system is provincially regulated in Saskatchewan and has no CER or FERC tariff; Plains posts tariffs for its crude systems on plains.com. Absence from tolls.db is a coverage and ingest item, never a market gap or an unpublished toll, and no toll number is invented.

No receipt-point list is drawn: WS-H publishes no tariff receipt-point list for the South Saskatchewan System (contrast the Westspur receipt terminals in the Kingston dataset). Fosterton is the one origin a source names for this corridor (Hansard 1955; Enbridge Commodity Map, effective 1 Oct 2025). Shaunavon, Dollard and Eastend country is catchment shading only; no receipt terminal is assigned to any of them.

The Regina to Co-op Refinery connection is not drawn as a pipeline: WS-H sec 8.4 states that a named, dedicated crude pipeline into the Co-op Refinery Complex, with an identified operator, route and capacity, was not established in the sources checked (WS-H open item 3). The local-market edge on this map is INFERRED, at city grain, and carries no route, operator or capacity claim.

The Wascana Pipeline is not drawn: it is a light-crude import feeder (U.S. Bakken via the Bakken North Pipeline to the border at Raymond, then 173 km north to the same PMC Regina Terminal, about 40 kb/d, CER profile via WS-H sec 1.4). It shares the delivery terminal but is not SW Saskatchewan heavy gathering; it belongs on its own map. Its absence here is scope, not a gap.
