Enbridge Mainline: committed 0 kb/d
Committed 0 kb/d; the entire system is uncommitted (3,050 kb/d on the egress stack marketable basis; 3,200 kb/d gross design nameplate, of which 215 kb/d is reserved for non-crude products). All Mainline capacity is uncommitted service under the Mainline Tolling Settlement (MTS): CER approved March 4, 2024; term July 1, 2021 to December 31, 2028. The Mainline contracting application was denied by the CER (RH-001-2020), so there is no committed table. The Mainline is not drawn on this figure; its geography is the egress dataset and its configuration is the Mainline schematic

Trans Mountain: committed 707.5 kb/d
Nameplate 890 kb/d; committed 707.5 kb/d and uncommitted 182.5 kb/d. Firm contracts are 15 and 20 year ship-or-pay with 13 shippers (93% of committed contracts are 20 year, escalating 2.5% per year). The firm cap rises from 80% to about 90% of nominal capacity (about 801 kb/d) on January 1, 2027 under the CER approved tolls settlement (Filing C41001, September 28, 2026); about 89 kb/d (10%) is the spot set-aside. Not drawn on this figure: its geography is the Trans Mountain dataset
tmx_network.json (trunk edge: committed_kbd 707.5, uncommitted_kbd 182.5, nameplate_kbd 890, commitment_term); egress_network.json (trans_mountain edge)

Keystone: committed 585 kb/d (AIF basis)
Committed about 585 kb/d, stated on the South Bow AIF basis: the AIF nominal basis is 591 kb/d, split 540 kb/d investment-grade and 45 kb/d non-investment-grade in the 2025 AIF. Long-term take-or-pay contracts, average 18 years from 2010. The egress stack nameplate (643 kb/d) sits on the marketable basis, a different basis from the AIF commitment: the two are never added or netted against each other, and no uncommitted_kbd is printed for Keystone. Not drawn on this figure: its geography is the egress dataset
egress_network.json (keystone edge: committed_kbd 585, commitment_term, nameplate_kbd 643); South Bow 2024 and 2025 AIFs via the house commitments record

Southern Lights: committed 165 kb/d through 2030
Design nameplate 180 kb/d; CER available capacity 198 kb/d since the Line 13 Expansion (July 2025). Committed 165 kb/d under the 2023 re-contracting open season TSAs through 2030, plus a 2024 expansion tranche (5 to 15 kb/d class). The original 15 year cost-of-service TSAs (90% committed, 10% uncommitted) expired June 30, 2025
egress_network.json (southern_lights_import edge); c5_network.json; tolls.db pipeline 12 notes (queried 2026-10-19 via python sqlite3)

Cochin: committed 90 kb/d (Class A and B), Class C undisclosed
Nameplate 110 kb/d (design and CER available capacity). Committed 90 kb/d in the 2023 Class A and B seasons: 3 to 6 year terms, expiries June 2027 to January 2030, in service August 1, 2024; 60 kb/d of the book was recontracted early in 2025 for terms up to 10 years. Class C subscribed volume is undisclosed, so 90 kb/d is a floor for the committed total, not the total
egress_network.json (cochin_import edge); commitments record: Pembina Q4 2023 release; CER No. 269 rules tariff; CER Cochin pipeline profile

Explorer: committed program minimum 60 kb/d (Item No. 118)
The diluent lane (Gulf Coast origins to Irwin and Manhattan, IL) runs under the Item No. 118 volume incentive program: a shipper holding a throughput-and-deficiency agreement in effect on or before January 12, 2025 commits a minimum of 60,000 b/d of diluent for an initial five-year term; program in service July 1, 2025; no priority capacity attaches (Item 118(h)). The 60 kb/d is the program minimum commitment, not a system committed total, and the tariff prints no diluent-lane nameplate
tolls.db pipeline 13 notes and tolls.id 272 to 286 (FERC No. 100.133.0, Item No. 118), queried 2026-10-19 via python sqlite3; c5_network.json (explorer edges)

Line 9: committed 260 kb/d of 300 kb/d
Nameplate 300 kb/d. Committed about 260 kb/d: Valero Levis about 130 kb/d plus Suncor Montreal about 130 kb/d, the only two shippers on the line. Uncommitted about 40 kb/d. The commitments are firm rights, not caps: either shipper may nominate above commitment on the uncommitted capacity

NOTES

Commitments are the layer on this dataset: no flows_kbd is carried on any edge. Flows live on the sibling datasets (the CER series on egress_network.json and line9_network.json; Southern Lights has quarterly snapshots only in the house CER file), and no flow is copied or re-keyed here.

Basis discipline: every committed volume is printed on the basis its source uses, and the basis is stated in its row. Keystone's 585 kb/d is an AIF-basis figure (591 kb/d nominal) and is never netted against its 643 kb/d stack-basis nameplate; Southern Lights is stated against both its 180 kb/d design nameplate and its 198 kb/d CER available capacity; the Mainline's stack figure (3,050 kb/d) and gross design nameplate (3,200 kb/d) are both printed rather than blended.

Design call (atlas build-list item 13): this dataset is an overlay, not a new geography. Coordinates and corridor waypoints are reused from c5_network.json and line9_network.json; Trans Mountain, Keystone and the Mainline appear as table rows only, because their geographies already have atlas datasets (tmx_network.json, egress_network.json, mainline_schematic.json) and redrawing them here would fork their numbers.

Overlay design: the figure redraws only the corridors where shipper commitments bind and that already have atlas geography: the C5 import corridors (Southern Lights, Cochin, Explorer) from c5_network.json and Line 9 from line9_network.json. Node coordinates and edge waypoints are reused from those datasets; the commitment fields are this dataset's primary content, and the print sheet extends the table to every committed system in the atlas plus the Mainline at committed zero.

Southern Lights nameplate basis: the edge carries the 180 kb/d design nameplate in nameplate_kbd, matching the sibling datasets; the CER available capacity of 198 kb/d since the Line 13 Expansion (July 2025) is stated in the edge source and the sheet row, because the schema carries one nameplate field and the siblings key it to design capacity.

Explorer commitment basis: the 60 kb/d on the Explorer trunk edges is the Item No. 118 program minimum commitment (tolls.db commitment level committed-explorer-diluent-118). The tariff prints neither a system committed total nor a diluent-lane nameplate, so neither is carried; the figure is a program floor. The commitment fields sit on the two trunk edges (Mont Belvieu to Tulsa, Tulsa to the Illinois cluster); the two short Gulf origin legs into Mont Belvieu carry no fields, as in the C5 drawing they are origin feeders.

Line 9 segment discipline: the system commitment split (260 kb/d committed, 40 kb/d uncommitted, 300 kb/d nameplate) is carried on the Line 9A origin segment, as in line9_network.json, and the two delivery legs carry each refiner's own 130 kb/d firm right. These are the same system barrels at successive points on one line, never additive.

No other atlas system is drawn or tabulated with a committed split it does not publish: Express, Milk River and Rangeland have no committed versus uncommitted split published in the house record (egress_network.json), so they appear nowhere in the table rather than with an invented zero. The Mainline's zero is different in kind: it is a published fact of the MTS framework, and it is printed as a first-class row.

WITHHELD

Cochin Class C subscribed volume is undisclosed: the committed figure printed (90 kb/d, Class A and B) is a floor for the system's committed total, not the total, and no Class C volume is estimated (egress_network.json commitments record; SCHEMA.md commitment-split rule: the undisclosed part of a split stays absent).

The Southern Lights committed full-path toll (Manhattan to Edmonton) has not been published since July 1, 2025: the US segment moved to a FERC index tariff when the original 15 year cost-of-service TSAs expired on June 30, 2025, and the two segments are now tolled separately (CER No. 27 on the Canadian segment, FERC No. 4.26.0 on the US segment). No through committed rate is printed; the segment tolls are cited on the edge (tolls.id 244 to 252). Per the C5 sheet precedent, the absence is printed, not filled.

Per-shipper committed splits are withheld on every drawn system: individual transportation service agreements are confidential. Only published aggregates are carried (Southern Lights 165 kb/d system total, Cochin 90 kb/d Class A and B, the Explorer Item 118 program minimum of 60 kb/d, and Line 9's two refiner volumes, which are House ground truth because the two refiners are the only shippers on that line).

Line 9 committed tolls are not in tolls.db: path 43 carries only the uncommitted tolls of Interim CER No. 587 (tolls.id 224, 225, 226), and no committed toll row is carried in the database, so none is printed. Per the standing tolls rule, a toll missing from tolls.db is an ingest backlog item, never a market gap (line9_network.json withheld list).

Explorer diluent-lane capacity is withheld: the tariff does not print a total diluent expansion capacity for the Item 118 lane (tolls.db pipeline 13 notes), so no nameplate_kbd is carried on the Explorer edges, and the 60 kb/d program minimum is never presented as a system capacity or a system committed total.

No uncommitted_kbd is printed for Southern Lights or Cochin: neither sibling dataset publishes a two-way committed and uncommitted split for these systems (Cochin's residual cannot be known while Class C is undisclosed), and a residual computed as nameplate minus committed would present arithmetic as a published split. Line 9 carries uncommitted_kbd because its split is published ground truth; Trans Mountain's split is published in its own dataset and is stated in the sheet row below.
