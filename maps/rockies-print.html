NOTES

Guernsey is a small town in eastern Wyoming doing a job out of proportion to its size: it is where the northern barrels (Bakken crude on Butte), the western barrels (Powder River and Casper crude) and the DJ Basin's overflow change trains. From Guernsey a barrel can become a Cushing barrel on Pony Express or a Midwest barrel on Platte.

The DJ Basin below it runs the opposite problem: three crude lines to Cushing with more capacity than Colorado fills, kept full by loading Wyoming barrels at their northern ends. And the one line people most often miscount, Front Range, is not crude at all: it is NGL, drawn dashed and labelled so it is never added to the crude doors.

Line width ranks the movements: width follows the published flow where one exists and capacity where only capacity is published. Every label states which it is.

Guernsey earns the hub treatment because four different stories meet there. Bakken barrels arrive from the north on Butte, a line whose filed nameplate (150 kb/d) its actual flows (317 to 340 kb/d in late 2025) left behind years ago. Wyoming barrels arrive from Casper and the Powder River. And what leaves Guernsey leaves in two directions: southwest-to-southeast on Pony Express to Cushing, or east on Platte to the Midwest. A barrel's price changes at Guernsey because its market changes there.

The DJ Basin's pipes are bigger than the DJ Basin. Saddlehorn, Grand Mesa and White Cliffs together carry about 560 kb/d of capacity against roughly 445 kb/d of Colorado production, less what Commerce City runs locally. The shortfall is made up with Wyoming and Bakken barrels loaded at the northern origin points, which is why Saddlehorn's origin list includes Cheyenne and Fort Laramie and why Plains keeps a Guernsey access point: the DJ lines stay full by importing somebody else's crude.

Front Range is on this map to be ruled out, not counted. It is an NGL line (250 kb/d, DJ Basin to Skellytown), drawn in the dashed gas style and labelled NGL. Anyone adding up DJ crude egress should count three lines only: Saddlehorn, Grand Mesa and the White Cliffs crude line.
