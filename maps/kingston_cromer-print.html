NOTES

Cromer is a legal and physical commingling point: on custody transfer to Enbridge, receipt commodities (SES, MSM) end and the shipper holds the equalized transport pool (LSB or Midale), settled through equalization WADFs.

The Saskatchewan Gathering and Weyburn systems are provincially regulated by the Saskatchewan Ministry of Energy and Resources; Westspur is the CER-regulated trunk they feed (CER Pipeline Profile - Westspur).

LSB and Midale are Enbridge equalized pools, not field grades: SES and MSM are the Kingston feeder streams, and the pools also take other Cromer receipts, so downstream pool quality is heavier and sourer than SES at the Westspur outlet (WS-H secs 4.2, 6.1-6.2).

Westspur also carried 2.8 kb/d of NGL in 2025 (CER profile). NGL is not drawn as a separate edge; every drawn edge carries product crude.

Enbridge's Bakken Pipeline (Line 26, approx 145 kb/d, CER profile) also delivers at Cromer and is not drawn on this Kingston map; its barrels arrive at the same Mainline receipt point and can enter the same pools (WS-H sec 2.2). Line 26 belongs on the Mainline/Bakken map.

Utilization, calculated: 152 kb/d in 2025 against approx 247.4 kb/d of system capacity is approx 61%. The mix is shifting: SES fell from 137 kb/d in 2018 to 104 kb/d in 2025, while MSM rose from 40.3 kb/d in 2016 to 45.7 kb/d in 2025 and 50.0 kb/d in the CER table's 2026 year-to-date column (CER profile, Aug 2026 update). Q2 2026 system throughput averaged 145.90 kb/d.

WITHHELD

Battery-level stream membership is not drawn and is not publicly derivable: in the held Petrinex test (June 2026), the battery-to-pipeline counterparty is confidentiality-suppressed on 87.9% of Saskatchewan and 78.9% of Alberta battery oil-disposition volume (WS-H Part 3). Catchment shading and feeder edges on this map are INFERRED at receipt-point grain, never battery membership.

Shipper-level committed split on Westspur: no committed or uncommitted kb/d total is published for the system. The CER profile describes market-based tolls with a volume and commitment discount structure, but no committed total, so committed_kbd and uncommitted_kbd stay absent on every edge.

Per-path Westspur toll levels are not printed: they are published in Kingston's filed CER tariff (Folder 3815522), but the full current toll table was not extracted for WS-H (WS-H open item 6) and tolls.db holds no Kingston/Westspur rows (queried 2026-10-19). Not in tolls.db is an ingest backlog item, never a market gap; no toll number is invented to fill it.

No monthly flow series (flows_kbd) is carried anywhere: the CER Westspur profile publishes annual average throughput for 2016-2025 plus quarterly 2026 figures, not a monthly series held by Heavy Reading. last_flow_kbd therefore carries the 2025 annual averages (system 152, SES 104, MSM 45.7 kb/d), and one held Petrinex month for Alida Terminal (June 2026). Nothing is interpolated between annual points.

The 2019 Settlement Agreement's economics are not printed: the blending revenue-sharing fee formula, the full eight-producer Producer Group membership and the settlement term are not public; the agreement was not filed with or approved by the NEB/CER and is known only through later CER evidence (WS-H sec 5.4). The map labels the MIW blending point and the contractual link to the Saskatchewan, Weyburn and Westspur toll settlements, and prints no fee.

Current butane injection volumes at the MIW are not published (WS-H open item 9); the approx 60,000 b/d of SES stream available for blending in the RH-003-2020 evidence is a 2019-vintage operational snapshot and is not printed as a current blending rate. The 95 kPa annual-average vapour pressure figure is likewise 2020 evidence vintage.

Cromer tankage by owner is not printed: Kingston's over 600,000 bbl of storage is a company-wide total across five terminals (Sep 2023) with no published terminal split, and no Enbridge Cromer Terminal tank count or capacity was verified from public sources (WS-H sec 4.4 and open item 3).

Total Cromer pool volumes are not printed: Enbridge does not publish LSB and Midale pool receipt totals at Cromer in the sources held (WS-H open item 5). SES and MSM are the Westspur feeds only; other Cromer receipts (Tundra Light Sour, Manitoba Light, Manitoba Medium, and Line 26 Bakken barrels) enter the same pools in unquantified shares (WS-H secs 6.1, 6.5).

Steelman butane blending is not drawn: blending facilities at Steelman and a Steelman Butane Line were raised as questions in Crescent Point interrogatories (2018) but not affirmatively confirmed in service in the record retrieved (WS-H sec 5.2 and open item 8). Only MIW/Cromer blending, which is affirmatively evidenced, is labelled.

The Secure Alida Terminal's current operating status after the 2022 CER settlement was not verified (WS-H open item 9). Its node stands for the documented Alida Interconnection receipt and delivery points directed into the Westspur Tariff on 22 Sep 2022, not as a statement about current blending operations at that terminal.

Saskatchewan Gathering and Weyburn line-by-line lengths and capacities are not printed: Kingston publishes system totals only (over 2,200 km of pipelines and five terminals, company-wide, Sep 2023). The approx 2016 Enbridge Income Fund anatomy (approx 1,600 km of gathering, approx 547 km of trunk) is a pre-sale vintage proxy, not Kingston's current asset register (WS-H sec 1.2 and open item 2).

Method: Facts compiled, expression ours (2026-10-17). Every node is a real place at gazetteer coordinates: receipt terminals sit at their towns; the Cromer node is plotted at the Enbridge Cromer Terminal, approx 2 km north of Cromer village (CER filing, SE 20-9-28 / NE 17-9-28 WPM), and the Manitoba Interconnect (MIW) node at the terminal site beside it (NW 17-9-28 WPM). Node existence and role are sourced per node from the WS-H gathering-systems dossier (stage1/ws_h_gathering_systems.md, dossier 2A and secs 8.1-8.3), which rests on the CER Pipeline Profile for Westspur and the CER RH-003-2020 hearing record. Edges connect nodes through corridor waypoints on or near the published route; gathering feeders, the Weyburn junction reading, the Manitoba gathering connection and both supply-area ellipses are INFERRED and render dashed. Edge system values are the WS-H system names verbatim. No flows_kbd series is carried: the CER profile publishes annual averages, not a monthly series held by Heavy Reading, and nothing is interpolated. tolls.db was queried 2026-10-19 (python sqlite3, tables pipelines/paths/tolls): it holds no Kingston or Westspur pipeline, path or toll rows, so no edge cites toll_refs; the only Cromer-origin path in tolls.db is Enbridge Mainline path 5 (Cromer Terminal to the international boundary near Gretna), a downstream Mainline path outside this Kingston dataset.
