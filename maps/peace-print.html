THE SYSTEM

Four products, each in its own line Fox Creek to Namao since Jun 30, 2017: ethane-plus, propane-plus, condensate, crude. Two tariff systems: LVP (crude + condensate) and HVP (ethane-plus, propane-plus, butane, condensate).

CAPACITY, kb/d

Peace + Northern combined (Pembina's basis)
≈1,100 (2024) → ≈1,200 with the Fox Creek to Namao expansion (Q1 2027)

Peace-only capacity: not published. Pembina discloses Peace only jointly with the Northern Pipeline; no Peace-only or crude-only figure exists in the public record. Do not quote one.
