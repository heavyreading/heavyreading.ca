NOTES

OPEN ITEM (WS-H, the single most consequential open item in the Rainbow dossier): how Peace River and Wabasca heavy oil historically associated with Rainbow is tendered at Edmonton today is not established in public sources. Enbridge's map shows Wabasca Heavy (WH) into the Conventional Heavy pool and Seal Heavy (SH) as a segregated commodity, but neither is attributed to Rainbow as its feeder in any document consulted. The possibilities (blended into the south leg, tendered as WH after batching, or no longer on Rainbow at all, with volumes moved to Pembina's separate Nipisi and Mitsue pipelines) are not resolved. No route for heavy tendering is drawn on this map.

HISTORY ELEMENT: the Norman Wells to Zama line (Enbridge Line 21) is drawn in grey dashes with no flow arrow and no live flow fields. Its last flow figure (4.7 kb/d, 2025 average deliveries to Zama) is a pre-shut-in vintage, printed with its year. Imperial shut in Norman Wells production in August 2026; whatever replaces that volume on Rainbow's north leg must come from Zama-area production itself.

Name collision warnings: Plains' Mitsue Terminal on Rainbow is not Pembina's Mitsue Pipeline, and Rainbow's Nipisi Terminal is not Pembina's separate 100,000 b/d Nipisi heavy pipeline (reactivated 2023). Pembina's Nipisi and Mitsue barrels reach Edmonton on Pembina's own Swan Hills corridor, not on Rainbow.

Rainbow II provenance is an open item: its build or conversion date, original purpose and current condensate volumes are not in the sources consulted. The condensate direction (Edmonton to Nipisi) follows the WS-H dossier reading of the 2026 brochure, which lists terminals and product without flow arrows.

Regulatory character: Rainbow is wholly inside Alberta and AER-regulated operationally, with no CER tariff. Tolls are commercial; see the withheld list for what is not printed and why.

OPEN ITEM: Peace River / Wabasca heavy feed tendering into this system at Edmonton is unresolved in public sources (WS-H open item 11). It is printed here and on the sheet as an open item; no route is drawn for it.

The Norman Wells to Zama edge is a history element: grey, dashed, no arrow, labelled shut in Aug 2026. Its nameplate (50 kb/d design) and last flow (4.7 kb/d, 2025) are historical figures with their vintages attached.

Geometry basis: facility coordinates are converted from the LSD legal locations in Plains' 2026 brochure (Alberta Township System conversion, atlas accuracy); town and area nodes use gazetteer coordinates. Routes run through the on-line facility coordinates where the brochure publishes them, so the trunk polyline passes through its own stations.

Current Zama-area plant operatorship (Paramount Resources succeeded Apache Canada in 2017, the last verified step in WS-H) and current Rainbow tolls are open items in the dossier and are not asserted on this map.

WITHHELD

Current Rainbow throughput and current rated capacity are withheld: the last system-specific figures in the public record consulted are 2007 average volumes (about 195,000 b/d) on about 200,000 b/d capacity at the 2008 acquisition. Plains reports Canada-region crude tariff volumes only in aggregate across all its Canadian systems (341,000 to 354,000 b/d in 2025 quarters); that aggregate is never attributed to Rainbow here.

Rainbow tolls are withheld: the system is intra-Alberta and AER-regulated operationally, with no CER tariff; historically it was not price-regulated by the NEB or any other body, and no current published Rainbow toll schedule was located in the WS-H pass (open item). tolls.db carries no Rainbow rows, so no toll_refs or toll_note is printed.

Norman Wells toll values are withheld: the line's tolls are CER-filed (Enbridge Pipelines (NW) 2024/2025 provisional toll filings), but no Norman Wells pipeline, path or toll rows are loaded in tolls.db yet. That is an ingest backlog item, not a market gap; the filed values will be cited by tolls.db id once ingested.

Committed vs uncommitted splits are withheld for Rainbow I and Rainbow II: no published split exists in the sources consulted, so committed_kbd and uncommitted_kbd stay absent on every Rainbow edge.

Rainbow II capacity is withheld: no capacity for the condensate leg (about 300 km, Edmonton to Nipisi) is disclosed in any source consulted.

Monthly flow series are withheld on every edge: no held monthly series exists for these systems, so flows_kbd is absent everywhere and nothing is interpolated. The only flow figures carried are dated annual points (2007 system average; 2025 Norman Wells deliveries), each with its vintage.
