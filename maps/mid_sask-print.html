NOTES

Disambiguation, in one line: if a source says Mid-Saskatchewan Pipeline, it means Inter Pipeline to Kerrobert. If it says Plains at Kerrobert, it means Manito. The two share a hub and nothing else in WS-H.

All Inter Pipeline figures on this sheet are 2020 vintage (AIF YE2020). Do not quote 150,100 b/d or 47,100 b/d without the vintage: Brookfield-era Inter Pipeline publishes no equivalent AIF (WS-H open item 10).

Catchment shading is inferred and receipt areas are named-area grain. Battery-level membership in this system is not publicly derivable (WS-H Part 3).

Which system this is, stated plainly: this sheet is the Mid-Saskatchewan Pipeline System, operator Inter Pipeline Ltd. WS-H sec 2.3 answers the brief's open question directly: Mid-Saskatchewan is Inter Pipeline, not Plains. It gathers Heavy Blend crude and Light Sweet crude from established fields near Kindersley and Kerrobert in west-central Saskatchewan and delivers to the Enbridge pipeline system at Kerrobert.

Which systems this is not (the name collisions, per WS-H): (1) Not Plains. Plains at Kerrobert is the separate Manito System (with the North Saskatchewan and Bodo / Cactus Lake pipelines), delivering to the PMC Kerrobert Terminal at the same hub; it is drawn on this map in its own line and system name, and WS-H assigns Kerrobert deliveries to both owners. (2) Not a region: Mid-Saskatchewan here is a named pipeline system, not central Saskatchewan geography and not the Saskatchewan River country. (3) Not either Saskatchewan Gathering System: WS-H records two of those, Kingston's in southeastern Saskatchewan (light and medium, feeds Westspur to Cromer) and Husky Midstream's at Lloydminster (heavy, Cenovus-operated, the WCS blender); neither is this system, and neither touches Kerrobert. (4) Not the South Saskatchewan System: that is Plains heavy gathering from the Fosterton country to Regina, on its own dataset.

Kerrobert is a shared hub, not a single-owner terminal: Inter Pipeline (Mid-Saskatchewan, to Enbridge), Plains (Manito, to the PMC Kerrobert Terminal, with approx 1 million bbl of storage and a unit-train rail terminal in WS-H sec 1.2) and the Enbridge Mainline itself all sit at Kerrobert. A note that attributes every Kerrobert barrel to one owner is wrong on the WS-H record. Cochin also passes Kerrobert (a station on its Saskatchewan segment, C5 dataset) and Southern Lights can deliver to Kerrobert; neither is a Kerrobert gathering system and neither is drawn here.

Every capacity and throughput figure for this system is AIF YE2020 vintage: ultimate approx 150,100 b/d and 2020 throughput approx 47,100 b/d, filed February 2021, before the Brookfield acquisition closed. There is no post-Brookfield AIF to update them with (WS-H open item 10); they are printed with their vintage, never as a current rating.

The 2014 expansion is the shape of the modern system: throughput had doubled to more than 70 thousand b/d in two years on Viking light-oil growth, and the expansion added approx 95,000 b/d (50-plus km of mainline, 40 km of laterals) in phases to mid-2015 (WS-H sec 2.3). Kerrobert storage was then expanded by 400,000 bbl in 2016. Both are history with their dates, not current flow figures.

WITHHELD

Battery-level membership is not drawn and is not publicly derivable: in the held Petrinex test (June 2026), the battery-to-pipeline counterparty is confidentiality-suppressed on 87.9% of Saskatchewan battery oil-disposition volume (WS-H Part 3). Receipt areas are shown at named-area grain only; catchment shading and the feeder edge are INFERRED, never battery membership.

Current (post-Brookfield) capacity is withheld, not restated: the nameplate and throughput carried here are Inter Pipeline AIF YE2020 figures (filed February 2021), the last public AIF. Brookfield-era Inter Pipeline does not publish an equivalent AIF, so no current re-rating exists in the record (WS-H open item 10). Both figures are printed with their 2020 vintage wherever they appear, never as current.

No monthly flow series is carried: flows_kbd is absent on every edge and nothing is interpolated between annual points. The only flow carried is the AIF 2020 annual throughput (47.1 kb/d) as last_flow_kbd with vintage 2020, following the landed conventional heavy dataset. The 2014 record in WS-H (throughput doubling to more than 70 thousand b/d in two years, before the expansion) is history in the edge source, not a flow series.

Committed and uncommitted capacity are absent: no committed split, committed total or commitment term for the Mid-Saskatchewan system is published in the sources checked, so committed_kbd, uncommitted_kbd and commitment_term stay absent on every edge. The storage figures (approx 798,600 bbl system storage; 400,000 bbl Kerrobert expansion in 2016) are printed in the print sheet; the schema carries no storage field and they are never converted into a capacity figure.

Tolls: no toll_refs are cited on any edge. tolls.db (queried 2026-10-19) holds no Mid-Saskatchewan or Manito pipeline, path or toll rows. Absence from tolls.db is a coverage and ingest item, never a market gap or an unpublished toll, and no toll number is invented. The only Kerrobert toll path in tolls.db is Enbridge Mainline path 3 from Kerrobert Station onward, which belongs to the Mainline dataset.

Manito capacity is absent because it is undisclosed, not estimated: no disclosed capacity for Plains' Manito system was found in the operator or CER sources WS-H checked (WS-H sec 1.2 and open item 2), so the Manito context edge carries no nameplate_kbd. Its diluent return lines and the Empress/Kerrobert diluent supply leg are described in its source and drawn on the conventional heavy dataset; they are not repeated on this small sheet.

No receipt-terminal list is drawn: WS-H sec 2.3 names the established fields near Kindersley and Kerrobert, not individual receipt terminals, batteries or pump stations for this system. Kindersley is drawn as a receipt area and Kerrobert as the delivery hub; anything finer would be invention. Fields near Kerrobert itself are inside the same receipt area and are not plotted as a second receipt node at the same place as the delivery hub.

No Mid-Saskatchewan stream name is printed: WS-H establishes the two gathered grades (Heavy Blend crude and Light Sweet crude, in the AIF 2020 throughput description) and the Mainline pools at Kerrobert as the downstream destination. No separate equalization stream named for this system was established, and none is assigned.
