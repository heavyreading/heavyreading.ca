NOTES

This is the largest oil field in the world drawn as a plumbing problem. About 6.8 million barrels a day come out of the ground 500 miles from the sea, and every barrel leaves through one of three doors: Cushing to the northeast, Houston to the east, Corpus Christi to the south.

The Corpus door is the one that matters most now: Gray Oak, Cactus II and EPIC together carry about 2.3 million barrels a day of capacity and run near full, because Corpus Christi is the top crude export port in the United States. The Houston door is bigger on paper and runs with room to spare.

Line width ranks the doors: width follows the published flow where one exists and capacity where only capacity is published. Every label states which it is.

Read the doors by where they point. Nothing out of this basin points north or west in size: the Cushing door (Basin and Centurion) is the smallest of the three, the Houston door carries about 3.3 million barrels a day of capacity, and the Corpus Christi door carries about 2.3 million and runs near full, because Corpus is where the export docks are. The basin's price problem is never the rock; it is which door is widest this quarter.

The production figures print at the grain the publishers actually finalize. Texas crude prints by RRC district (District 8 at 2,434 kb/d of crude in June 2026, preliminary, crude only); the county series the RRC posts is a first release that revises upward for months, so this map does not print county crude. New Mexico publishes complete county data, so the Delaware's New Mexico side prints at county grain: Lea and Eddy at 2,326 kb/d in August 2026.

Local refining is a rounding error on this map by design: Big Spring and El Paso together run 206 kb/d of crude capacity against about 6.8 million barrels a day of production. The Permian is an export basin that happens to have two refineries in it.

WITHHELD

County level Texas crude: the RRC county series is published as a first release and revises upward for months (its July 2026 Reeves County crude print is a fraction of the county's filed production). This map prints the RRC district series, the finest grain the Commission finalizes, and says so on the label.

Monthly flows by pipeline: no public source publishes them. Form 6 is annual, so flows on this map print at Form 6 grain only.

The gas and NGL side of the basin: excluded by design. Waha gas egress and the NGL lines are a separate map; nothing on this page is a gas or NGL line.
