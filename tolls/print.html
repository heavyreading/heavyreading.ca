Data vintage: 2026-10-15

A toll is the price a pipeline charges to move oil from where it enters the line to where it is delivered, charged per barrel or per cubic metre. Every toll on this sheet comes from a tariff the pipeline filed with its regulator, so any reader can check the figure against the filing itself.

Method and sources: tolls compiled from filed tariff documents of the Canada Energy Regulator and the US Federal Energy Regulatory Commission; the source filing and effective date ride with every record on the Tolls Search page. Tolls are printed in the units the tariff files them in. Charges a tariff lists separately from the toll, such as surcharges the record names as excluded, are not in these figures; the full record states what each toll includes and excludes.
