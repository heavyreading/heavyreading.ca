Middle East watch

One war, three fronts: Iran's sanctioned exports under the naval blockade, tanker movement through the Strait of Hormuz, and Houthi pressure on Red Sea shipping. The fronts move together; this page tracks them together. Every number is either tracker-observed or claimed, never both; bullets say which. When any front tightens, buyers bid up substitute sours and the whole complex firms, supporting Canadian differentials; when they loosen, medium and heavy sours soften.

Read this page in order: what changed, what it means for barrels, how it reaches the market, and what could escalate next. Front 1 (The war) covers Iran's sanctioned exports, floating storage, the shadow fleet, and China's buying. Front 2 (Hormuz) covers tanker movement through the chokepoint against its pre-war baseline. Front 3 (Red Sea) covers Houthi pressure on Bab el-Mandeb shipping, Saudi export rerouting, and the Cape of Good Hope diversion. The archive at the bottom holds the blockade timeline and baselines.
