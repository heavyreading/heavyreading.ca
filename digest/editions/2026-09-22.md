## The call
- **Saudi East-West restart CONFIRMED** (Reuters, this morning). Yanbu exports could resume later Tuesday — first cargo to China, at a low rate — with the line targeting ~4 mb/d. Brent fell $2+ to its **lowest since September 8**.
- **WTI October expired today.** Monday's settle was $95.78, down 4.51% — the fourth straight down day, roughly **-$10 over four sessions**.
- **Tuesday's Asian Brent prints conflict**: one set shows a diplomacy bounce above $101, another shows -2.46% at $97.87 on an unverified Kyodo report of Hormuz reopening. One of them is wrong; don't trade the gap until it's resolved.

## Geopolitics through a supply/demand lens
- **Hormuz escalation.** A tanker was struck by a projectile on Sep 21 (UKMTO); CENTCOM redirected 109 vessels and traffic fell to ~3 crossings a day. A US-sanctioned Chinese tanker crossed despite the blockade.
- **Trump–Pezeshkian.** Trump addressed UNGA this morning — reportedly in "deciding mode," likely open to a meeting with Iran's president. Tehran laid out 7 conditions via Qatar.
- **Trump–Zelensky met today** on an energy-infrastructure truce; the Russia/Iran sanctions tariff law is signed — up to 100% tariffs on the top buyers of Russian oil, effective within 30 days.
- Ukraine's Sep 19–20 drone barrage on Moscow was its largest of 2026; the Moscow refinery (~245 kb/d) was reportedly hit — the claim is **unverified**.

## Canada: refineries
- **BP Whiting returned to normal operations Monday** (Reuters); USW talks are today after a roughly six-month lockout, with a strike authorized.
- **Joliet**: still no confirmed restart. Power was resolved Sep 18, but no restart date has been given and the IIR restart estimate has lapsed.

## Cracks & positioning

- Brent managed-money net length: **282,657 contracts** (+16.9k, highest since May, as of Sep 15). WTI around 106k — one source reports ~118k on the same dataset, so treat the level as approximate.
