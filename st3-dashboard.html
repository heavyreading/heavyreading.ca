Q3 2026: nowcast bridge

September 2026 is the nowcast bridge and carries two turnarounds, both modeled down at the leaves. The Horizon 35-day turnaround began September 8 (CNRL 2026 budget guidance, 2025-12-16); September takes a -230.0 kb/d bridge at the Horizon plant leaf per the editorial ruling of 2026-09-22, which allocated 6,900 kb of the guidance-implied 10,585 kb to September. The Syncrude Coker 8-2 turnaround has been down since its planned August 20 start (Suncor Q2 2026 MD&A, 2026-08-04; deferred from Q2 per the Imperial Q2 2026 call); September is a full outage month at -77.0 kb/d on the Syncrude Mildred Lake upgrader leaf, and August carried the Aug 20-31 ramp at -29.8 kb/d. The mine dials back with the upgrader at -13.4 kb/d in August and -34.7 kb/d in September at the Syncrude Mildred Lake mined leaf (45% of the SCO rate, approved 2026-09-23), and the coker feed shortfall that is not dialed back is assumed redirected to the Suncor Base upgrader via the interconnect: a positive SCO offset of +7.2 kb/d in August (capped at the 380 kb/d demonstrated Base headroom) and +49.3 kb/d in September on the Suncor OSG upgrader leaf. Imperial's Cold Lake 3Q/4Q turnaround contributes -3.0 kb/d in each month (Imperial 2026 corporate guidance, annualized rate). All August and September figures are nowcast estimates built 2026-09-23, not AER published data.

Grade-layer assumptions

Named assumptions behind the grade rows and the Coker 8-2 disposition. Guidance-sourced and pattern-indicated maintenance are kept separate throughout.

Horizon-style, no interconnect
Symmetric mine/upgrader dial-back. Horizon and Scotford have no documented third-party interconnects, so their upgrader outages are modeled as straight dial-backs. Confirmed 2026-09-23.
Suncor/Syncrude upgrader-only event
45% Syncrude mine dial-back, balance redirected through the interconnect, receiving SCO offset subject to Base headroom. The 45% is the midpoint of the 30-60% empirical range in annotations/maintenance_disposition_history.md (11 documented integrated-site events, 2022-2026). Approved 2026-09-23.
PFT mines (Kearl, Fort Hills)
Output flows to the highest-netback outlet. Proven by Fort Hills holding output flat (+4%) in Q1 2026 while redirecting 66.9 kb/d from dilbit sales to Base upgrader feed.
SAGD
Output unchanged by another asset's upgrader outage; the upgrader-bound share is assumed diverted to dilbit. MacKay River held at -5% during the Q2 2026 Suncor upgrader outage.
Default upgrader yield
0.87 SCO per barrel of bitumen feed. Observed context: fleet 0.865 (ST3, stable 2017-2026), Horizon 0.888, Syncrude 0.843 (repo-computed from AER ST3/ST39, 2026-09-23).
Base headroom cap
380 kb/d demonstrated headroom ceiling for the receiving SCO offset (Q1 2026 precedent: Base ran at ~380 kb/d, about 108% of ~350 nameplate, while processing 16.4 kb/d Syncrude bitumen and 66.9 kb/d Fort Hills bitumen).
Limitations
No project-level SAGD/PFT disposition shares are invented. Where the ST3 lines cannot support a leaf-level dilbit split, the grade is the ST3 residual and the leaf split is labeled null, never plugged. The uncaptured August redirect remainder is labeled, not plugged.

How to read this

Actuals run Aug 2025 through Jul 2026 (AER ST3). Aug-Sep 2026 (amber) are nowcast estimates: each line's seasonal baseline plus confirmed maintenance events, built 2026-09-23; they are not AER published data. Oct 2026 (blue) is the last context month, post-nominations. The tradable window (green) runs Nov 2026 onward: that is the barrel that prices. Bright orange cells mark modeled maintenance offline: the Maintenance offline memo row, its per-event child rows, and every project or plant cell modeled down for an outage. A dark badge on a deducted leaf cell shows the kb/d hit; a positive badge (for example on the Suncor Base upgrader leaf in Aug-Oct 2026) shows a modeled redirect offset; tap either for the source and reasoning. The grade rows at the bottom of the table cut the same supply by grade instead of by source: SCO (which equals upgraded production), marketable bitumen (dilbit), bitumen sent for further processing (a memo, shown positive), and the grade total, which ties exactly to Total WCSB crude supply every month. See the grade-layer assumptions panel below for the named assumptions and the leaf-to-grade mapping. are AER ST53 commercial-scheme forecasts, grossed up by 1.27 so they reconcile to the ST3 in-situ line (mapped projects cover 79.0% of ST3 in-situ; the remainder sits on the "Unmapped in-situ" row). Mined bitumen and SCO drill into AER ST39 facility rows (actuals through 2026-05; Jun-Jul 2026 blank until AER publishes), with plant forecasts scaled to tie exactly to the ST3 lines. Every row carries a Why note explaining the reasoning behind its forecast: named maintenance events with dates and kb/d impacts, decline assumptions, project ramps, and where a single-month dip is just the seasonal factor repeating. Confirmed turnarounds are modeled down at the affected project and plant leaves (orange cells with a kb/d badge). Maintenance offline is a memo row that details those same outages (tap +): confirmed turnarounds with kb/d impacts, plus pattern-indicated 2027 windows that carry no volume. Implied storage change is a model output with no actuals counterpart. Unknown values are blank, never filled in.
