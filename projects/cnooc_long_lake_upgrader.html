DescriptionLong Lake is an integrated SAGD and upgrader complex about 40 km southeast of Fort McMurray. The upgrader was idled in 2016 after a fatal explosion in January 2016; since then, the SAGD operation has sold diluted bitumen directly to market and the project functions as a SAGD-only producer. This file covers the complex; the ST53 scheme "Long Lake" tracks the SAGD bitumen line.
Source: Oil Sands Magazine, The Oil Sands Weekly, 2016-07-15
Source: Alberta Energy Regulator, CNOOC Long Lake and Kinosis annual performance presentation, 2021-06-30
StatusOperating as a SAGD-only scheme under scheme approval 9485 (as amended). The upgrader is idled; a restart application was filed in November 2021 and its status is not disclosed in reviewed sources.
Source: Alberta Energy Regulator, CNOOC Long Lake and Kinosis annual performance presentation, 2021-06-30
Source: CPECN/Bloomberg, Long Lake Upgrader To Be Restarted Six Years After Fatal Explosion, 2022-01

The SAGD facility is separately reported with capacity of 90,000 bbl/d. Note: secondary-sourced.
