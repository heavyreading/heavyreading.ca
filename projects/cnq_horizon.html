DescriptionHorizon is an integrated oil sands mining and upgrading project north of Fort McMurray. Mined bitumen is upgraded on site to synthetic crude oil (SCO). Canadian Natural reports three distinct capacity bases for Horizon: mined-bitumen capacity, operating SCO capacity, and regulatory SCO nameplate. These are different figures and must not be mixed.
Source: Oil Sands Magazine, CNRL Horizon Mine project profile, 2026-09-23
Source: Canadian Natural Resources Limited, Q1 2025 interim report, 2025-05-08
StatusOperating. The NRUTT (Naphtha Recovery Unit Tailings Treatment) project is sanctioned with a Q3 2027 in-service target.
Source: BOE Report, Canadian Natural Resources Limited announces 2026 budget, 2025-12-16
Nameplate capacityMined-bitumen capacity of 314,000 bbl/d.
Source: Oil Sands Magazine, CNRL Horizon Mine project profile, 2026-09-23

SCO operating capacity of 264,000 bbl/d, following the 2024 Reliability Enhancement Project.
Source: Canadian Natural Resources Limited, Q1 2025 interim report, 2025-05-08

Regulatory SCO nameplate of 271,000 bbl/d. Note: secondary-sourced.
Source: Oil Sands Magazine, 2026 production forecast: oil sands mining and upgrading, 2026-01-15

Demonstrated peaks exceed all three stated bases: 309,800 bbl/d SCO in November 2025 and 333,300 bbl/d mined bitumen in May 2026 (AER ST39, "CNRL HORIZON OIL SANDS PROJECT", Production). The model's capacity reference for headroom/utilization math uses these demonstrated peaks; the three stated bases are recorded as stated. Per the standing rule, where actuals beat announced capacity, history wins. Mine-bitumen capacity and upgrader SCO capacity are kept as separate lines, never blended.
Start-up / phasesnot tracked in the public data used here
Ownership / JV splitCanadian Natural Resources Limited owns 100% of the Horizon mine and upgrader and is the operator. Canadian Natural acquired the remaining working interests in the Athabasca Oil Sands Project assets from Shell and Marathon, and since March 1, 2025 it holds 100% of the Albian mines (Jackpine and Muskeg River). It also holds an 80% non-operated interest in the Scotford Upgrader (Shell 20%, operator), a separate upgrading asset.
Source: Canadian Natural Resources Limited, acquisition announcement, via BOE Report, 2017-03-09
Product and dispositionSCO from the upgrader; mined bitumen to the upgrader (froth treatment NFT: upgrader-locked, cannot meet pipeline dilbit spec)
Source: annotations/maintenance_disposition_history.md

Market relevance

Horizon SCO averages 282.7 kb/d over the forecast. Horizon has no documented third-party interconnect, so the model dials mine and upgrader back together during turnarounds: SCO supply tightens one-for-one with the modeled outage. Modeled maintenance peaks at Oct 2026 (+119.0 kb/d).
