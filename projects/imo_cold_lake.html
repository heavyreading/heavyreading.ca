Market relevance

In-situ bitumen has flexible disposition (dilbit to market or upgrader feed). In the model, Cold Lake output is unchanged by upgrader outages and any upgrader-bound share is assumed diverted to dilbit, so its 199.5 kb/d 12-month forecast average is a steady contributor to marketable dilbit across the tradable window.
