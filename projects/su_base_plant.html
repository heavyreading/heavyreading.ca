DescriptionBase Plant (Oil Sands Base) is Suncor's integrated mining and upgrading complex north of Fort McMurray. Mined bitumen is upgraded on site to synthetic crude oil (SCO). It is part of Suncor's Oil Sands Operations segment and connects to the Syncrude and Fort Hills assets via bi-directional interconnecting pipelines brought into service in Q4 2020.
Source: Suncor Energy, Suncor reports fourth quarter 2020 results, 2021-02-09
Source: Suncor Energy, Q4 2025 investor presentation

Base Mine production was 262,000 bbl/d of bitumen in 2025.
Source: Suncor Energy, Q4 2025 investor presentation
