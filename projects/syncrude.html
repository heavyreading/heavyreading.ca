DescriptionSyncrude is an integrated oil sands mining and upgrading joint venture in the Athabasca region (Mildred Lake and Aurora mines, upgrader). Suncor assumed operatorship in October 2021. Mined bitumen is upgraded on site to synthetic crude oil (SCO).
Source: International Mining, Suncor officially takes over operatorship of Syncrude Joint Venture, 2021-10-08
StatusOperating.
Nameplate capacityGross SCO capacity of about 350,000 bbl/d.

Source: Oil and Gas Journal, Suncor buys Mocal Energy's Syncrude stake, 2018-02-12

Suncor reports its share of upgrader capacity as 206,000 bbl/d, net to its 58.74% working interest (206,000 / 0.5874 implies about 350,700 bbl/d gross; the company does not state the gross figure, so record both bases without treating them as a conflict).
Source: Suncor Energy, Q4 2025 investor presentation

Syncrude gross production averaged 323,000 bbl/d in 2023 (190,000 bbl/d net to Suncor).
Source: Suncor Energy, Suncor Energy achieves 2023 upstream production corporate guidance, 2024-01-03
Start-up / phasesnot tracked in the public data used here
Ownership / JV splitThe joint venture partners are Suncor Energy Inc. (58.74%), Imperial Oil Resources Limited (25.0%), Sinopec Oil Sands Partnership (9.03%), and CNOOC Oil Sands Canada (7.23%). Suncor's interest grew from 53.74% to 58.74% when it closed the purchase of Mocal Energy's 5% interest on February 26, 2018, effective January 1, 2018. Suncor is the operator (since October 2021).
Source: Suncor Energy, Suncor Energy closes purchase of additional interest in Syncrude, via GlobeNewswire/BOE Report, 2018-02-26
Source: International Mining, Suncor officially takes over operatorship of Syncrude Joint Venture, 2021-10-08
