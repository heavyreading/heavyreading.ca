What flips this

  • Enbridge apportionment jumps past 20% on the next CER print.
    Flips it: Egress flag goes from WATCH to active constraint; basis bearish signal strengthens.
  • A 2027 turnaround gets announced for a major mining or SAGD asset.
    Flips it: Forward supply drops in that month; the flat storage path steepens into a draw.
  • August ST3 actuals print well above the July run rate.
    Flips it: Supply baseline resets higher; November/December balances build faster than +191/+166.
  • PADD 2 refinery runs fall back under seasonal norms into fall turnaround season.
    Flips it: Demand-side flag turns; removals need to absorb more or storage builds.

Methodology and sources

Physical supply/demand balance, not a differential forecast. Supply forecast at granular project leaves (AER ST53 commercial schemes for SAGD/CSS, ST3 lines for mined and conventional), then aggregated: trailing-12-month mean times a seasonal factor from the last three complete years. Mapped ST53 projects cover 76.7% of ST3 in-situ; the balance is grossed up with an explicit coverage factor.

Maintenance offline counts only public-confirmed company guidance and transcript items. Seasonal-pattern indicators are shown as assumed and never enter the math. Unknown values stay null.

Implied storage change = supply total minus maintenance offline plus diluent imports minus refinery demand minus removals (all kb/d). It is the simple physical balance, not the ST3 inventory print.

Flag signals are regime reads, not forecasts. No storage/differential relationship enters any model until point-in-time tests beat naive carry.
