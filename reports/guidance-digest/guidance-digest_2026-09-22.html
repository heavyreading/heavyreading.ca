The call

  • Q3 2026 was the heavy maintenance quarter and it is settled or behind us: Syncrude -77 gross, Horizon Sep -230, Christina F/G done.
  • Q4 2026 guided drag is roughly -145 kb/d on a mixed basis (Base Plant -20, Cold Lake -6, Horizon Oct residual -119). Cenovus guided Q4 Oil Sands maintenance to zero.
  • Nothing is guided for 2027 inside the window. Cycle extensions are the theme: Kearl to 4-year intervals (next 2029), Firebag to 5-year (next ~2031), Christina F/G 2031, Horizon next 2028.
  • Corporate actions move the balance more than turnarounds this quarter: Greenfire closed Connacher (current ~34 kb/d vs 21.5-23.5 guidance); IPC Blackrod ramping to a stated 30 kb/d plateau by end-2027.
  • Standing disclosure gaps: ConocoPhillips/Surmont, Harvest/BlackGold, CNOOC/Long Lake give nothing forward. Silence is recorded, not omitted.

What changed vs last quarter

Horizon Sep/Oct 2026 turnaround settled in the model: Sep -230 (actuals bridge), Oct -119 (approved residual); next 2028.

Syncrude Coker 8-2 deferred from Q2 to Q3 2026 (-77 gross / -45 net Suncor). Kearl moved to 4-year intervals; Firebag to 5-year.
