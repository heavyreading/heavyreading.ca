What flips this

  • Unplanned upgrader outage (Suncor/Syncrude trip)
    Flips it: The historical shock: offline barrels arrive with no guidance lead time and the monthly commentary reprices immediately.
  • Suncor publishes its 2027 maintenance table with heavy spring work
    Flips it: The empty Jan-Sep 2027 column fills in; the light-calendar read reverses.
  • A pattern window confirms with a company-stated volume
    Flips it: MacKay River September is the one to watch; confirmation moves it from ASSUMED into the math.

Methodology and sources

Source types: guidance (company stated), transcript (call quotes), seasonal_pattern (repo-computed from AER ST53), bloomberg_recon (Bloomberg First Word 2026 schedule via desk photo, not public).

A record enters the headline only when the company stated both the number and the timing (validation public-confirmed) or guidance with an approved editorial ruling (Horizon October residual).

Seasonal method: for each scheme, monthly bitumen vs trailing 3-month mean; a month flags when production dips more than 15% below that mean. A calendar month is pattern-indicated when it flagged in >=4 years and >=30% of observed years (ramp-up excluded).

Full lineage per record (company, document, date, URL, quote) lives in annotations/maintenance_forward.yaml.
