Reading the calendar

  • Public confirmed: the company stated the number and the timing. Only these enter model math, and only after approval.
  • Guidance sourced: verified from company guidance or transcripts (e.g. positive statements of no turnaround).
  • Pattern indicated: the seasonal study flags the month as a likely maintenance window. Indicators only: no volume, never in the math.
  • Unknown volumes and dates stay null, never guessed.
