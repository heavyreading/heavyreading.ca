Shipper Commitments

Committed (contracted) capacity on long-haul crude pipelines out of Western Canada, by shipper

v1written September 30, 2026research vintage Sep 28-30, 2026public data only

Most long-haul crude pipelines out of Western Canada are majority-contracted: shippers hold long-term firm capacity (take-or-pay or ship-or-pay) and the toll is the price of the commitment. The Enbridge Mainline is the anomaly - zero percent contracted, every barrel on spot service. This grid records disclosed committed volumes by shipper. Per-shipper splits are confidential by design (transportation service agreements carry non-disclosure terms), so most cells are dashes. A dash is a finding about the public record, not a claim that no commitment exists.

Honest gaps

  1. Per-shipper TMX volumes for 9 of the 11 current shippers (BP, CNRL, ConocoPhillips Canada, Imperial, Marathon, Parkland, PetroChina, Suncor, plus one unnamed 11th shipper). Only Cenovus (~144 kb/d, press-grade) and the MEG 20 kb/d (filing-grade, now Cenovus's) have public volumes. The 707,500 b/d aggregate and the 2013 name list are filing-grade; the split was never published.
  2. The 11th current TMX shipper is unnamed in every source found.
  3. Travel of legacy positions (Husky to Cenovus, Devon to CNRL, Tesoro to Marathon, Total to unknown) has no public record; marked inferred or unknown, never assumed.
  4. Equinor's current TMX status: in the original 13, absent from 2024 lists, no disclosed sale found. Unknown.
  5. Keystone: committed share known (~585 of 591 kb/d), individual shippers largely not found beyond the confirmed names.
  6. Flanagan South and Seaway: per-shipper committed volumes beyond the Cenovus/MEG 100 kb/d not published.
  7. Southern Lights: per-shipper split of the 77 kb/d not published.
  8. Express-Platte, diluent lines (Waupisoo, Norlite, Polaris), AOSPL, Cochin, Spearhead, Marketlink, Capline, Ozark: no per-shipper committed volumes surfaced; operators do not publish per-shipper lists.
  9. The smaller producers (Baytex, Whitecap, Vermilion, Strathcona, Obsidian, Tamarack, Cardinal, InPlay, Headwater, IPC) disclose only dollar-denominated transportation commitments, never per-pipe volumes. A genuine absence in the public record, not a search failure.
  10. The summary band (Nameplate / Spot / Net Committed) is filing- or press-grade per pipe; do not confuse it with the shipper cells below, which are far sparser.
  11. An unconfirmed 'Tesoro Canada 508 kb/d' TMX figure circulated in an earlier research brief; it could not be verified in any proceeding or filing and is not carried on the page.
  12. The underlying CER and FERC docket exhibits (beyond decisions, tariffs, and releases) were not exhaustively searched; a redacted shipper-level breakout could exist in a non-public filing.

About this page

Committed capacity is the contract layer of the egress system: who has locked in space, for how long, on what commercial structure. It sits under the toll database - the toll is what the commitment costs. Figures here are researched from company filings (annual information forms, 10-Ks), regulatory proceedings (CER, FERC, NEB), and energy press reporting company or official statements, each tagged. Nothing here is modeled or estimated except where labeled.
