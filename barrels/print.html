How much crude Western Canada produces in marketable form, whether the pipelines can move it, and what is left for storage. All figures are in thousand barrels per day (kb/d) unless a row says otherwise. Every number on this sheet is computed from the same data as the Barrels page, at the same time, so the two cannot disagree.
